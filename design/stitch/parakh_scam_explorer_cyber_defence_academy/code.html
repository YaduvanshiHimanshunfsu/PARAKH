12 Common Scam Types: Modus Operandi & Red Flags प्रचलित 12 साइबर ठगी के तरीके एवं उनसे बचाव के सूत्र

warning Critical Severity SCAM-01
power

Electricity Bill Disconnection

बिजली बिल रुकावट ठगी

Modus Operandi / कार्यप्रणाली:

SMS claiming electricity will be severed at 9:30 PM tonight due to unpaid bills. Urges calling a personal 10-digit mobile number or installing an APK screen-share tool.

crisis_alert 3 Immediate Red Flags:
  • Sent from personal 10-digit SIM (not official Discom DLT header like JVVNL)
  • Demands ₹10 token recharge via suspicious APK file
  • Fake urgency: "Power cut tonight at 9:30 PM"
verified Discom never sends personal APK links
gavel High Extortion SCAM-02
videocam_off

Digital Arrest Impersonation

डिजिटल अरेस्ट धोखा

Scammers pose as CBI/ED/Cyber Police in staged fake police stations via Skype/WhatsApp video, claiming your Aadhaar was found in illicit parcel narcotics, confining you to room for hours.

priority_high Sovereign Law Fact:

There is NO provision of "Digital Arrest" under Indian law (CrPC / BNSS). Police NEVER arrest or settle cases over video calls.

Immediate action: Disconnect & dial 1930
account_balance Banking Phish SCAM-03
credit_card_off

Bank Account KYC / PAN Freeze

बैंक केवाईसी सस्पेंशन

SMS warning "Your SBI/HDFC account will be blocked today due to pending PAN/KYC update. Click here to verify". Leads to spoofed banking portals designed to harvest login credentials and OTPs.

visibility Spot The Clue:

URLs end in .club, .top, .live instead of certified onlinesbi.sbi

Banks never send KYC update links
smartphone Blackmail APK SCAM-04
currency_rupee

Predatory Instant Loan Apps

अवैध लोन ऐप जालसाजी

Unregulated loan APKs promising 2-minute collateral-free loans exfiltrate contacts and gallery photos, followed by morphed extortion calls to your family within 7 days.

Permission Checklist:

Genuine RBI NBFC loan apps never demand read-access to contacts, SMS, or camera rolls.

Check RBI Sachet Registry
thumb_up Work From Home SCAM-05
send

Telegram Task & Like Scam

यूट्यूब लाइक / टास्क फ्रॉड

Offers ₹150 for rating hotel reviews or liking YouTube videos. Scammers pay small amounts initially to build false trust, then trap victims into ₹50,000+ "crypto prepaid task" escrow traps.

Psychological Hook:

"Sunk cost trap" — Victims keep depositing more money believing it is necessary to unfreeze previous earnings.

Real companies don't hire on Telegram
local_shipping Delivery Smishing SCAM-06
inventory_2

Courier & Parcel Hold Trap

कूरियर / पार्सल रुकावट

Fake India Post / FedEx SMS stating your parcel cannot be delivered due to wrong house address. Demands a nominal ₹25 address change fee to capture debit card credentials and OTP.

Telltale Sign:

Official India Post URL is indiapost.gov.in, not short links like bit.ly/in-post24

Never pay delivery fees via SMS link
traffic Malware Attack SCAM-07
directions_car

Fake Traffic Challan / E-Court

ई-चालान फर्जी लिंक

Text claiming an over-speeding violation on Jaipur-Delhi highway with photo proof. The link downloads Challan_Notice.apk which forwards all your phone's SMS and OTPs to hacker servers.

Official Verification:

Check real challans directly on echallan.parivahan.gov.in only.

Court notices never arrive via APK
trending_up Institutional Ponzi SCAM-08
query_stats

Investment WhatsApp Group Fraud

शेयर बाजार फर्जी ट्रेडिंग

VIP WhatsApp group with fake "SEBI registered analysts" sharing doctored screenshots of 500% intraday gains. Directs users to custom fraudulent apps where dashboard balances can never be withdrawn.

Regulatory Standard:

Verify advisor registration number on SEBI.gov.in. Real trading requires a CDSL/NSDL Demat account.

Guaranteed returns = Guaranteed Scam
fingerprint Biometric Cloning SCAM-09
contactless

AePS Biometric Cloning Leak

आधार बायोमेट्रिक फ्रॉड

Fingerprint impressions copied from land registry registries or fake CSP kiosks are cast in polymer to pull cash daily via AePS micro-ATMs without requiring bank OTPs.

1-Minute Defence Shield:

Open mAadhaar app > Lock Biometrics. Unlock only when physically present at a bank counter.

Lock biometrics today via mAadhaar
psychology Psychological Trauma SCAM-10
video_camera_front

Video Call Sextortion Trap

न्यूड वीडियो कॉल ब्लैकमेल

Victim receives a WhatsApp video call showing an explicit clip. The screen is recorded with the victim's face in the thumbnail, followed by threats from fake "Delhi Police Cyber Cell" to circulate it on YouTube.

Police Advisory:

Do NOT pay even ₹1. Paying leads to endless extortion demands. Block number and file report immediately on cybercrime.gov.in.

Confidential support via 1930
favorite Social Engineering SCAM-11
redeem

Matrimonial Romance Gift Scam

मैट्रिमोनियल कस्टम गिफ्ट स्कैम

A profile posing as a doctor or engineer from the UK/Dubai proposes marriage on Shaadi/Jeevansathi, then claims to have sent costly jewelry held at Delhi Airport Customs, requiring ₹85,000 "customs release fee".

Customs Regulation:

Indian Customs never calls individuals demanding duty transfers into personal savings accounts.

Never pay customs into private UPI
qr_code_scanner UPI Reversal SCAM-12
qr_code_2

QR Code "Receive Money" Scam

क्यूआर कोड पेमेंट स्कैम

Fraudster on OLX/Marketplace pretends to purchase your used furniture, sends a QR code stating "Scan this QR and enter your 4-digit UPI PIN to receive ₹12,000 advance payment".

security Golden UPI Rule:

UPI PIN is entered ONLY to SEND money, NEVER to receive money!

Scanning QR = Approving Outflow

Police Safety SOP

Golden Protocol (कार्रवाई दिशा-निर्देश):

  1. Zero Compliance: Never transfer "verification token amounts" or click SMS web links.
  2. Isolate Connection: If an APK was downloaded, immediately put the phone in Airplane mode.
  3. Report Immediately: Call Rajasthan Police Helpline 1930 within the "Golden Hour" (first 2 hours).
  4. Preserve Evidence: Do not delete call logs, WhatsApp chats, or screenshot strings.
