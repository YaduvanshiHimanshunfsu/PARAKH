Electricity Bill Disconnection

बिजली बिल कटने की फर्जी धमकी (Discom SMS)

"Dear Consumer, your electricity power will be disconnected tonight at 9:30 PM from Jaipur Discom office because your previous month bill was not updated. Please immediately contact our electricity officer Mr. Sharma at 9829XXXXXX."
flag Red Flag: Private 10-digit mobile number given instead of official Jaipur Vidyut Vitran Nigam portal.
shield Safety: Discoms never send disconnection warnings via personal WhatsApp or mobile numbers.
EXTREME DANGER videocam_off

Digital Arrest Video Call

डिजिटल अरेस्ट फ्रॉड (CBI / Police Impersonation)

"You are under Digital Arrest under Section 120B IPC for a parcel sent to Taiwan containing 16 passports and drugs. You must stay on video call with Cyber Crime Branch officer until funds in your account are verified."
flag Red Flag: Indian Law has NO provision for "Digital Arrest" via Skype, WhatsApp, or video calls.
shield Safety: Disconnect immediately and inform Rajasthan Police at 1930 without panic.

Fake RTO E-Challan APK

फर्जी ट्रैफिक चालान एपीके फाइल

"RJ-14-EA-XXXX Challan Pending! Your traffic violation fine Rs.1000 is overdue. Download Traffic_Challan_Court.apk to pay before court warrant is issued: bit.ly/rj-traffic-pay"
flag Red Flag: Downloading an .apk file instead of routing to the official echallan.parivahan.gov.in portal.
shield Safety: Never install application files sent via SMS or messaging apps.

Predatory Loan Apps

flag Red Flag: Requests full access to Contact List, Camera, and Gallery upon installation.

Part-time YouTube / Hotel Tasks

यूट्यूब लाइक व होटल रेटिंग टास्क फ्रॉड

"Earn ₹2500 - ₹5000 daily from home just by rating 5 hotels on Google Maps. We already paid ₹150 for your trial task. Join Telegram receptionist @HR_Pooja to claim bonus."
flag Red Flag: Small payouts initially to gain trust, followed by demands for ₹50,000 "crypto merchant deposit".
shield Safety: Legitimate companies never charge pre-payment or security deposits for jobs.
