Chronological Recovery Checklist / क्रमबद्ध सुरक्षा प्रक्रिया

PHASE 1: IN THE NEXT 15 MINUTES (अगले 15 मिनट के भीतर)
Priority: IMMEDIATE
Call your bank's 24/7 fraud helpline to freeze your account Step 1.1

Tell them: "My account has suffered an unauthorized cyber debit. Put an immediate debit-freeze and block net-banking access."

Quick Bank Hotlines: SBI: 1800 11 1109 HDFC: 1800 258 3838 ICICI: 1800 1080 PNB: 1800 180 2222
Reset UPI PIN from an independent clean device Step 1.2

If your phone had any screen sharing app active, do NOT type your PIN on it. Open PhonePe/GPay/BHIM on a family member's clean phone or call customer care to deregister UPI handles.

Record 12-digit UPI UTR number and transaction SMS Step 1.3

Check your bank debit SMS. Copy the 12-digit numeric code starting with UTR / Ref No: 4192XXXXXXXX. This is essential for the 1930 operator to trace the receiver's intermediary wallet.

PHASE 2: WITHIN 1 HOUR (1 घंटे के अंदर)
Priority: HIGH
Lodge formal ticket at cybercrime.gov.in under 'Financial Fraud' Step 2.1

Submit the incident on the National Cybercrime Reporting Portal. Select Rajasthan as state. Note down the generated 14-digit acknowledgement number for your police FIR.

Purge screen-sharing utilities or unfamiliar downloaded APKs Step 2.2

Uninstall AnyDesk, RustDesk, QuickSupport, or any recently installed APK. Put phone in Airplane Mode if unexpected popups or automated taps are occurring.

Change linked primary email & password credentials Step 2.3

Reset your Google/Apple account password and revoke all active sessions ("Log out from all devices") from a trusted laptop or desktop.

PHASE 3: WITHIN 24 HOURS (24 घंटे के भीतर)
Recovery & Legal
Present acknowledgement at nearest Rajasthan Cyber Police Station Step 3.1

Carry printed copies of your 1930 / cybercrime.gov.in acknowledgement slip, bank account statement with marked debits, and suspect chat screenshots to convert your entry into a registered FIR.

Demand written Lien / Debit Freeze confirmation from Bank Manager Step 3.2

Submit a physical letter with your branch manager citing RBI Circular RBI/2017-18/15 regarding zero liability for timely reported unauthorized electronic banking transactions.

Re-issue replacement cards and clean Net Banking credentials Step 3.3

Ensure the compromised plastic card is hot-listed permanently, not merely paused, and apply for a freshly minted card number.
